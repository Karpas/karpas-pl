# KarPas / Form follows curiosity

Direction: experimental, physical, expressive. Acid yellow, violet, ink, polished fluid surfaces. Polish language, independent maker rather than fictional agency.

## Experience
- First frame: oversized editorial typography, sculptural violet/chrome knot, lime field, compact studio navigation.
- Interactive material: liquid knot that responds to pointer and changes shape. A visible motion toggle; respect reduced motion.
- Scroll chapter: a sticky 3D scene decomposes from a fluid idea into layers and back into a finished form. Three deliberate beats, native scrolling.
- Capabilities: an asymmetric editorial composition with technical line illustrations and clear scope.
- Material lab: visitor selects PLA, PETG, TPU; physical appearance and descriptive properties change together.
- Calculator: actual workspace with sticky model preview, stepped controls and live estimate. Keep existing price formula and STL parsing; make uploads render as actual geometry, not a decorative approximation. Label illustrative geometry. Quantity, material, add-ons and dimensions remain editable.
- Ending: oversized direct contact with a purposeful path into the calculator.

## Validation
Desktop 1280+; phone 390 and 320; keyboard navigation; reduced motion; WebGL failure; default, bulk, invalid and uploaded STL calculations; production build; no external CDN dependency for runtime graphics.

## Avoid
Generic card grids, fake client proof, fictitious delivered products, scroll hijacking, loading theater, screenshots that hide broken lower sections, unreadable type over graphics.
